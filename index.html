How this actually works

The part real casinos leave out.

Every round on this site is decided by one function — settle() in js/core/rig.js — before any animation starts. The reels, the pegs, the wheel and the toilet are all just theatre replaying a result that already exists.

The distribution is public: about 62% of rounds pay you nothing, about 33% pay back a fraction of your stake — usually between 0.1% and 35% of it — and roughly 0.5% are genuine wins. That last half a percent is not generosity, it is the hook. A machine that never paid out would be abandoned in a minute.

The presentation is deliberately decoupled from the result. Any payout above zero triggers the full jackpot sequence: sirens, confetti, screen shake, a rolling gold counter. Win 0.01 credits off a 50-credit stake and the screen will scream JACKPOT at you, with your actual net loss printed underneath in 8px grey.

And when you lose, you nearly won first. Every time. The near-miss is generated on 82% of total losses because near-misses light up the same part of your brain as wins do. That is not our invention. That is the industry.

None of the above is exaggerated. The only thing this site does differently is print the numbers on the box.
